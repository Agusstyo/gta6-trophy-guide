    What next? The planner

    A hunt stalls when everything left looks equally far away, so the planner ranks the open trophies for you, from your own saved progress. Counters you have started come first, nearest their PlayStation target at the top, with exactly how much is left. Then the watch-list trophies still unearned, because a one-off moment is worth meeting prepared rather than by accident. Then the quick wins — unearned, spoiler-free trophies the guide rates as a single short job. The list rebuilds itself every time you tick a trophy or move a counter, and every row opens that trophy's guide.

    The road to Vice Got a Price!

    Six stages, in the order we would play them. The staging is built from the official trophy descriptions alone: story first because the story trophies are a straight line, strangers and partner work while the world is fresh, then the great open-world sweep, then the money, and the 100% mop-up last because almost everything else feeds it. Stage progress is read from your tracker.

    The watch list — read this before the endgame

    Honesty first: no trophy on the official list is confirmed missable. The list's descriptions do not say that any opportunity closes, and this game lets nobody check yet. What follows is a watch list, not a warning label: the few trophies whose wording suggests a one-off moment, a unique target, a choice, or a second attempt — the shapes of trophy that can be tied to a single chance. Each entry explains the reasoning, so you can judge it yourself. Everything not listed here shows no missable risk in its description at all.
